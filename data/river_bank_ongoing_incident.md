# River Bank Ongoing Activity / ஆற்றங்கரை தொடர் நடவடிக்கை

**App:** SelfTrust  
**Nature:** Repeated resource appropriation from commons  
**Context:** River bank water pumping operations

---

## 🎯 PPDTF Analysis

### 👥 PEOPLE

#### Offender
- **Role:** Individual/commercial water extractor
- **Intent:** Irrigation or business water needs
- **Law Intent Break:**
  - Extracts water without permit or limit
  - Privatizes public water resource

#### Facilitator
- **Role:** Pump suppliers and diesel vendors
- **Intent:** Equipment provision
- **Law Intent Break:**
  - Enables unauthorized extraction

#### Witness
- **Role:** Local community members
- **Intent:** Normalize as "farming need"
- **Law Intent Break:**
  - Does not question permit status

#### Enforcement Authority
- **Role:** Water Resources / Revenue officials
- **Intent:** Regulate water extraction
- **Law Intent Break:**
  - No monitoring of cumulative extraction
